# Code of conduct

Flyleaf is for families and the people who help them record their history.
Everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere
else the project meets, should be able to do so without harassment or
disrespect.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Many people here are new to code, to genealogy, or
  to both.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you judge.
- Give and take criticism of work, not of people.
- Respect privacy. Never post a real family's tree, settings, photographs or
  relatives' details, yours or anyone else's, without their consent.
- Credit other people's work.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Discrimination of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information, such as an address or a
  relative's details, without their permission.
- Any other conduct a reasonable person would find unwelcome in a friendly,
  public space.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, contact the maintainer privately,
through the contact details on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Say what
happened, where, and when. Reports are kept confidential.

## What happens next

The maintainer reads every report, and may ask for more information. Then
the maintainer decides what to do, and may remove comments, code or issues,
or block someone from the project, for as long as is needed. Anyone who is
asked to stop should stop at once.
