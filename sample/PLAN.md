# Plan

The Penhallow family's plan. This family is fictional: it shows what a plan
looks like after a few months of work.

## Goal

A family edition of the book for the Penhallow and Solberg cousins at
Christmas, and a private website they can sign in to. A printed edition
the year after.

## Priorities

1. Every direct ancestor back to the great-grandparents has a record for
   birth, marriage and death.
2. Finish the Penhallow line's stories: John and Elizabeth's crossing, the
   mining years at Mineral Point, the move to Dodgeville.
3. Record more of Ruth Solberg's memories of the Solbergs.
4. Gather the photographs the cousins hold.

## Standing decisions

- Research budget: up to the price of two certificates per session. The
  assistant proposes each purchase and what it would show; Carol pays.
- Living relatives: public-record facts only, plus what each person agreed
  to share.
- The website stays private, behind sign-in.
- Tamsin reviews every chapter before it is marked final.
