## 2026-10-14: The Treloars in 1930

**Question:** What was Mary Treloar's maiden name? It would open her family,
and Joan's recipe booklet says Mary taught her to bake.

**Sources checked:**
- United States census of 1930, Mineral Point, the Treloar household: found
- The undated typed list of Treloar names, among Joan's papers: read again

**Found:**
- Thomas Henry Treloar, a miner, at Mineral Point in 1930, with Mary and
  their children. Cited on `thomas-treloar` (residence, occupation), QUAY 3.

**Not found:**
- Mary's maiden name. The census does not ask for it. The typed list names
  her only as Mary.

**Conflicts:** None.

**Open questions:**
- Read the full 1930 entry for Mary's age and her parents' birthplaces.
  Added to research/questions.md.

**Records to request:**
- The 1922 marriage entry for Thomas Henry Treloar, from the county register.
  Added to research/requests.md.

**Confidence changes:** None.

**Copies saved:**
- None. The census page is already attached to the 1930 census source.

**Commit:** None; the sample family is not a repository.

## 2026-10-12: Richard Penhallow in St Just

**Question:** Were John Penhallow's parents Richard and Grace Penhallow of
Trewellard, as his baptism says? Entering them opens the Cornish generation.

**Sources checked:**
- St Just in Penwith burial register, 1845–1868, Penhallow and Penhallo: not
  found
- John's 1866 marriage entry, read again: Richard Penhallow is a witness

**Found:**
- Nothing new for the tree. The witness may be John's father, but the entry
  does not say so.

**Not found:**
- No burial of Richard Penhallow in St Just from 1845 to 1868. He may have
  died after John emigrated, or been buried elsewhere. Recorded as a
  `[NEGATIVE]` note on `john-penhallow`.

**Conflicts:** None.

**Open questions:**
- A second record naming John's parents, such as the 1851 census of
  St Just. Already in research/questions.md.

**Records to request:** None.

**Confidence changes:** None.

**Copies saved:** None.
